03Questions

Before you ask.

What does Juan José Henao do?

I’m an AI video editor for paid social. I edit short-form ads, UGC-style ads, podcast clips and personal-brand reels for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Meta ads. Edit, motion graphics, AI B-roll and sound design come in one delivery.

Who is this for?

Startups, mid-size and large companies, and the agencies that serve them, when they run paid social and need fresh ad creative every week instead of one video a quarter.

What do I get in a delivery?

A finished vertical video ready to run: hook on the first frame, cuts on full phrases, word-level captions, motion graphics for every claim, B-roll and a mixed soundtrack. The project file stays editable, so changes don’t mean starting over.

How do you use AI?

For speed and for shots that would otherwise need a shoot: AI B-roll with Seedance and Kling, avatars and voice with HeyGen and ElevenLabs, and my own pipeline that finds silences, aligns captions and audits every cut. The taste and the final check are mine. Anything showing an AI-generated person is labelled as such.

How do we work together?

On a monthly retainer. You send raw footage or a brief, I send back finished ads, and what the numbers say shapes the next batch. I’m remote, based in Medellín, Colombia (UTC−5), and work US business hours. English and Spanish.

How do I start?

Email me or find me on LinkedIn. Or start with a free tool: score your current ad with the Frame-Zero Checklist, pull hooks from the Hook File, or ask for ten ad ideas written for your brand.
